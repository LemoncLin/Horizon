## [Graduate Student Proves Quantum Uncertainty Principle for Fractals](https://www.quantamagazine.org/graduate-student-proves-the-fractal-uncertainty-principle-20260812/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Graduate student Alex Cohen has proven the fractal uncertainty principle, a foundational result that bridges quantum mechanics, chaos theory, and infinitely complex fractal structures. The proof establishes a new uncertainty principle relating to fractals—shapes that remain equally complex no matter how much you zoom in. This is a major mathematical breakthrough with implications across multiple fields, including quantum chaos and spectral theory. The result provides deeper understanding of how quantum systems behave on fractal structures, potentially influencing both pure mathematics and theoretical physics. The fractal uncertainty principle was originally conjectured by Bourgain and Dyatlov in 2016. Cohen's proof has already proven influential across mathematics, and the principle connects to how wave functions behave on fractal sets through the Fourier transform.

rss · Quanta Magazine · Aug 12, 14:14

**Background**: In quantum mechanics, particles are described mathematically as waves, with the Fourier transform connecting a particle's position description to its momentum description. The classical uncertainty principle states that position and momentum cannot both be known with arbitrary precision. Fractals are geometric shapes that exhibit self-similar complexity at every scale. The fractal uncertainty principle extends these ideas to functions whose support is concentrated on fractal sets, placing fundamental limits on simultaneous localization in both position and frequency.

## [All-Optical Photonic Time Crystal Created with Terahertz Light](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02478-5) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have demonstrated an all-optical photonic time crystal by delivering intense terahertz light onto a microstructured material that hosts plasmonic collective electron waves, modulating its refractive index over time. This breakthrough introduces a novel all-optical approach to temporal optical engineering, with potential implications for advancing photonic devices and optical computing technologies. The photonic time crystal was achieved by using intense terahertz light to modulate the refractive index of a microstructured material hosting plasmons, which are collective oscillations of free electrons.

rss · Nature · Aug 12, 00:00

**Background**: A photonic time crystal is an optical system whose properties, such as refractive index, are periodically modulated in time rather than space. Time crystals were first proposed in 2012 by Alfred Shapere and Frank Wilczek as a time-based analogue to conventional crystals, where atoms are arranged periodically in space. Plasmons are collective oscillations of free electrons in metals or semiconductors, and their integration with time-modulated optics opens new pathways for controlling light.
