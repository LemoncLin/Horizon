## [NVIDIA Invests $7B in Poolside to Build Open-Source AI Models](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/nvidia-is-spending-6-billion-to-build-a-powerful-u-s-alternative-to-chinese-ai-c51c38cc) ⭐️ 8.0/10

NVIDIA is investing $1 billion at a $12 billion pre-money valuation in AI startup Poolside and paying an additional $6 billion for technology licensing, bringing over 100 engineers to advance its Nemotron open-source model project. This $7 billion total investment positions NVIDIA to build one of the world's most powerful open-weight models, directly competing with Chinese open-source models like DeepSeek and Kimi K3, as well as US closed-source players like OpenAI and Anthropic. Poolside is an AI startup focused on coding automation and software development tools, having raised $626 million total. The deal combines equity investment with technology licensing, and the Nemotron project includes open datasets, training recipes, and evaluation frameworks released under permissive licenses.

**Background**: Nemotron is NVIDIA's family of foundation models, including large language models and reasoning models, along with associated open datasets and developer tools. The open-source AI model race has intensified with Chinese models like DeepSeek and Kimi K3 leading the open-weight rankings, prompting major US tech companies to push back against regulatory restrictions on open model distribution.

## [Apple's First Foldable iPhone Set for September Launch at Over $2,000](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-23/apple-s-foldable-iphone-details-retail-store-changes-for-new-home-products-mt5vjf61) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple's first foldable iPhone is expected to launch around September 9 with a price over $2,000, but it will lack a telephoto camera and use Touch ID instead of Face ID, according to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman. This marks Apple's long-awaited entry into the foldable phone market, a major product category expansion that could reshape the premium smartphone landscape and influence consumer adoption of foldable devices. The foldable iPhone will lack a telephoto camera and use Touch ID instead of Face ID. Apple also plans to raise iPhone 18 Pro prices by $100 to $1,199 and is adjusting retail store layouts for new smart home products.
