## [Apple Introduces M6 and M5 Ultra Chips](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/08/apple-introduces-m6-and-m5-ultra-for-a-big-leap-in-performance-and-ai-compute/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple announced the M6 chip, its first 2-nanometer processor, debuting in the new Mac mini, and the M5 Ultra, its most powerful chip ever, launching in the refreshed Mac Studio. The M5 Ultra features a groundbreaking quad-chip architecture with up to 36 CPU cores, 80 GPU cores, and memory bandwidth reaching 1.2TB/s. This announcement marks a significant leap in Apple Silicon performance and AI compute capabilities, reinforcing Apple's competitive position in the premium computing market. The M6's 2nm process and M5 Ultra's quad-chip design demonstrate Apple's continued innovation in custom silicon, impacting both consumers and the broader tech industry. The M6 features a 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU, dual 16-core Neural Engines, and up to 170GB/s memory bandwidth. The M5 Ultra supports up to 512GB unified memory with 1.25x higher single-threaded and 1.3x higher multithreaded performance than M3 Ultra. Pricing starts at 6,999 yuan for Mac mini M6 and 12,999 yuan for M5 Pro, with the fully configured Apple Studio reaching up to $24,699.

**Background**: Apple Silicon refers to Apple's custom ARM-based processors that replaced Intel chips in Mac computers starting in 2020. The M-series chips have progressively improved performance and efficiency with each generation, introducing features like the Neural Engine for AI workloads. The transition to 2nm process technology represents a major manufacturing milestone, while the quad-chip design in M5 Ultra allows for unprecedented core counts and memory bandwidth in a single package.

Apple has announced a new Mac Studio powered by M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips, featuring Thunderbolt 5 connectivity, up to 1.2TB/s internal memory bandwidth, and a focus on local AI workloads. Pricing remains a contentious topic, with users noting the high cost of memory upgrades. This announcement marks a significant upgrade for Apple's professional desktop line, targeting creators and AI developers with enhanced local processing capabilities. The integration of Thunderbolt 5 and higher memory bandwidth could set new standards for external connectivity and data throughput in professional workstations. The Mac Studio offers up to 1.2TB/s internal memory bandwidth and Thunderbolt 5 with 120Gb/s external I/O. Pricing for high-memory configurations is expected to be substantial, with 256GB memory starting around $10,000.

## [OpenAI's Custom Jalapeño Chip Outperforms Nvidia Blackwell in Benchmarks](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI has released first test data for its custom inference chip Jalapeño, co-developed with Broadcom, showing 1.5 to 1.9 times more AI work per watt and 1.7 to 3.6 times lower latency than Nvidia's GB300 across three large language models. This marks a significant shift as top AI labs move toward in-house hardware, potentially reshaping the AI chip market, reducing reliance on Nvidia, and impacting token pricing and infrastructure economics. The chip is rated at 700W TDP but sustains under 550W, is optimized for inference rather than training, and was developed in just nine months from design to tape-out, with deployment planned by year-end and second and third generations already in progress.

hackernews · bmulholland · Aug 25, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49434378)

**Background**: Custom silicon, or ASICs (Application-Specific Integrated Circuits), are chips designed for a single purpose, offering higher efficiency than general-purpose GPUs for targeted workloads. AI labs like OpenAI and Anthropic are increasingly building their own inference chips to reduce costs and gain control over their infrastructure as model sizes and deployment scale grow.
