## [SK Hynix Raises Record $26.5B in US ADR Listing Amid AI Chip Demand](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/10/south-koreas-sk-hynix-raises-26-5bn-in-record-breaking-us-ipo) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On July 10, SK Hynix listed its American Depositary Receipts on NASDAQ at $149 per share, raising approximately $26.5 billion and surpassing Alibaba’s 2014 record for foreign companies. The offering was oversubscribed more than seven times, driven by surging demand for high-bandwidth memory chips. This massive capital raise highlights the critical role of South Korean semiconductor manufacturers in the global AI hardware supply chain. It signals a major shift in investor confidence toward AI infrastructure components, particularly memory solutions that alleviate GPU processing bottlenecks. SK Hynix remains the world’s leading manufacturer of high-bandwidth memory, supplying essential chips to NVIDIA and AMD’s latest AI graphics processors. The stock opened at roughly $170 on its first trading day, reflecting strong market appetite despite potential manufacturing constraints.

**Background**: High-bandwidth memory utilizes a three-dimensional stacking architecture connected by through-silicon vias, which vertically link multiple DRAM dies to a base logic chip. This design significantly increases data transfer speeds while reducing power consumption, effectively solving the memory bottleneck that traditionally limits AI accelerator performance. As artificial intelligence models grow increasingly complex, the demand for such advanced memory has strained global manufacturing capabilities, making production capacity a critical industry constraint.
