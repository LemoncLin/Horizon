## [Anthropic's Claude Formalizes Fermat's Last Theorem in 11 Days](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02822-9) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Anthropic's Claude generated a 13-million-line computer-checked formal proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in just 11 days, with the work published in Nature. This achievement marks a major milestone in AI-assisted mathematics and formal verification. This represents a paradigm shift in AI-assisted mathematics, demonstrating unprecedented capability in automating complex mathematical proof formalization. It could accelerate mathematical research and verification across disciplines by reducing the manual effort required to formalize proofs. The proof was verified using Lean, a proof assistant that algorithmically checks logical correctness. The 13-million-line output represents a complete formalization of the theorem, which previously took human mathematicians decades to develop.

rss · Nature · Sep 7, 00:00

**Background**: Formal verification involves using computer proof assistants like Lean to algorithmically verify mathematical proofs, ensuring correctness beyond doubt. Fermat's Last Theorem, conjectured by Pierre de Fermat in 1637, was famously proven by Andrew Wiles in 1994 after centuries of effort. Formalizing such complex proofs traditionally requires extensive manual work by mathematicians and computer scientists, making this AI-generated formalization a significant breakthrough.

## [Huawei Unveils Kirin 9050 Pro with Novel 3D Logic-Folding Architecture](https://www.news.cn/20260907/adf46c5c003240d28cc3cf6de54f9b5f/c.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On September 7, Huawei unveiled the Kirin 9050 Pro chip in its Mate XT 2 tri-fold phone, marking its first high-performance Kirin chip launch in six years and introducing a novel 3D logic-folding architecture with vertical interconnect channels. This launch demonstrates Huawei's ability to innovate beyond traditional semiconductor scaling, potentially reshaping the competitive landscape in mobile processors and reducing reliance on advanced lithography for performance gains. The Kirin 9050 Pro features a redesigned CPU with one prime core and two performance cores, a GPU that is 142% faster than its predecessor with ray tracing support, and a logic-folding architecture that shortens critical paths by 50%-80% while increasing transistor density to 238MTr/mm².

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 7, 08:20

**Background**: Traditional chip design has relied on shrinking transistors to fit more on a single die (Moore's Law), but as physical limits are approached, manufacturers are exploring alternative approaches. 3D stacking involves placing multiple chip layers vertically connected by through-silicon vias (TSVs), while Huawei's 'logic folding' represents a different approach that restructures the internal circuit topology of a single chip die rather than simply stacking separate dies.
