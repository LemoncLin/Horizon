## [DeepMind's WeatherNext Breakthrough in Cyclone Forecasting](https://deepmind.google/blog/weathernext-ai-model-achieves-breakthrough-in-forecasting-cyclones/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

DeepMind's WeatherNext AI model has achieved a breakthrough in cyclone forecasting, providing an extra day of warning accuracy, and the company is now open sourcing the model. This advancement continues the trend of specialized AI models outperforming traditional numerical weather prediction systems, which could significantly improve disaster preparedness and save lives in cyclone-prone regions. The WeatherNext model is based on multi-scale hierarchical Graph Neural Networks and delivers forecasts that are both more accurate and orders of magnitude more efficient than classic NWP models during inference.

hackernews · bhavansig · Aug 8, 09:18 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49220126)

**Background**: Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) has long been the standard for meteorological forecasting, using complex physical equations to simulate atmospheric conditions. Recent AI approaches like Graphcast have demonstrated that graph neural networks can model weather patterns more efficiently by representing the Earth's surface as a graph. WeatherNext builds on this foundation with specialized architecture for cyclone prediction.
