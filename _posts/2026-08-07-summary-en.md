## [Chinese Scientists Confirm Existence of Glueball, a New State of Matter](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Chinese scientists leading the BESIII collaboration have experimentally confirmed the existence of a glueball, a particle composed entirely of gluons bound together. The team identified the particle X(2370) in 2011 and, after 15 years of research, measured its quantum state properties and decay modes in 2024, finding them consistent with glueball predictions. This is the clearest experimental confirmation of a glueball in nearly 50 years of searching, marking a landmark validation of the Standard Model of particle physics. It demonstrates that gluons — the carriers of the strong nuclear force — can bind together to form a new state of matter, as predicted by quantum chromodynamics. The research was conducted at the Beijing Electron Positron Collider using the BESIII detector. The team measured multiple new decay modes of X(2370) and determined its flavor-singlet nature, which is a key signature distinguishing glueballs from ordinary quark-antiquark mesons.

**Background**: The Standard Model of particle physics describes the fundamental building blocks of matter and the forces that govern their interactions. Gluons are the force carriers of the strong nuclear interaction, which binds quarks together inside protons and neutrons. Quantum chromodynamics (QCD), the theory of the strong force, predicts that gluons themselves carry color charge and can interact with each other, potentially forming bound states called glueballs. Despite being predicted since the 1970s, glueballs have proven extremely difficult to identify experimentally because they can mix with ordinary mesons, making them hard to distinguish from conventional quark-antiquark particles.
