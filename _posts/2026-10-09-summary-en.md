## [Breaking down nerve-cell scaffold relieves chronic pain in mice](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03159-z) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers discovered that breaking down nerve-cell scaffolds can relieve chronic pain in mice, according to a study published in Nature on October 9, 2026. This research could help develop painkillers that are safer and more effective than existing treatments, representing a significant advance in pain management. The study, published in Nature, demonstrates that targeting nerve-cell scaffolds offers a new approach to chronic pain treatment, potentially leading to better painkillers.

rss · Nature · Oct 9, 00:00

**Background**: Chronic pain affects millions of people worldwide and is often difficult to treat with existing medications, which can have significant side effects. Nerve-cell scaffolds, also known as perineuronal nets, are specialized extracellular matrix structures that surround neurons and play a role in stabilizing neural circuits. These scaffolds are involved in plasticity and have been linked to chronic pain conditions.
