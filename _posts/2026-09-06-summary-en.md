## [OpenAI Reflects on AI Alignment Challenges in 'An Alien Mind' Blog Post](https://openai.com/index/an-alien-mind/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The post has sparked active discussion on Hacker News with 209 comments. ## [German Spectrum Rocket Achieves First Orbital Launch from European Soil](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/09/german-company-becomes-first-in-europe-to-launch-fully-commercial-orbital-rocket/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

German startup Isar Aerospace successfully launched its two-stage Spectrum rocket from Norway's Andøya Space Center, placing 5 small satellites and an experimental payload into low Earth orbit, becoming the first privately developed rocket to achieve orbital flight from European soil. This milestone gives Europe autonomous access to space, reducing dependence on foreign launch providers and strengthening the continent's position in the growing small-satellite launch market. The 28-meter-tall Spectrum rocket was designed to carry up to 1,000 kilograms to low Earth orbit at a target cost of €10,000 per kilogram, with the launch occurring at 22:12 local time from the Arctic Circle facility in northern Norway.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 6, 13:32

**Background**: Isar Aerospace was founded in 2018 by three students from the Technical University of Munich and is based near Munich in Ottobrunn. The company has been developing the Spectrum rocket as Germany's first privately developed orbital launch vehicle, aiming to provide affordable access to space for small satellites. The Andøya Space Center in northern Norway serves as a strategic European launch site at 69° north latitude, offering favorable orbital inclination options for polar and sun-synchronous orbits.
