## [OpenAI's Astra Model Breaks Through on 10 Long-Standing Math Problems](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

OpenAI's internal Astra model has made progress on ten long-unresolved mathematics and theoretical computer science problems, including high-dimensional sphere packing, the existence of non-sofic groups, and a disproof of Connes' rigidity conjecture. The AI-generated arguments were organized into papers and formally verified in Lean through human-AI collaboration. This marks a significant milestone in AI-assisted mathematical research, demonstrating that large language models can contribute to deep, open problems that have resisted progress for over a decade. It signals a potential shift in how mathematical discovery is conducted as AI becomes a research collaborator. The proofs were generated by AI with a token cost of approximately $2,000, while humans were responsible for organizing the arguments and formalizing them in Lean. OpenAI acknowledges that the mathematical arguments themselves are AI-generated and advocates for transparent attribution reflecting the actual sources of the results.

**Background**: Lean is a proof assistant and functional programming language based on the Calculus of Inductive Constructions, a foundational type theory. It has become a central tool for formal verification of mathematics, with a growing community library called Mathlib. The ten problems addressed by Astra span areas including geometric topology, operator algebras, computational complexity, and combinatorics — fields where automated theorem proving has historically struggled to make meaningful progress on open conjectures.

## [EA to Complete $55B Sale to Saudi Consortium on August 4](https://www.gamersky.com/news/202607/2180618.shtml) ⭐️ 9.0/10

EA has received all regulatory approvals for its $55 billion acquisition by a consortium led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF), Silver Lake, and Affinity Partners, with the deal expected to close on August 4, 2026. Upon completion, EA will become a private company and its financial data will no longer be publicly disclosed. This is the second-largest gaming buyout in history, trailing only Microsoft's $75.4 billion acquisition of Activision Blizzard in 2023, and will significantly reshape the gaming industry landscape. The deal also marks a major expansion of Saudi PIF's gaming portfolio, which already includes stakes in Electronic Arts, Take-Two, Activision Blizzard, and full acquisitions of Scopely and Niantic. The $55 billion deal values EA at $210 per share, representing a 25% premium. If completed, PIF alone will own more than 93.4% of EA, making it the largest investor in the transaction. The acquisition was announced on September 29, 2025.
