## [Astronomers have confirmed an atmosphere on the rocky exoplanet LHS 1140b](https://hackaday.com/2026/07/20/earth-like-lhs-1140b-may-feature-the-first-atmosphere-found-on-exoplanet/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers have identified an atmosphere around LHS 1140b, a rocky super-Earth located in the habitable zone of a red dwarf star 49 light-years away. The detection, reported through observations of escaping helium, marks the first confirmed atmosphere found on a relatively rocky exoplanet in this region. This discovery represents a major milestone in astrobiology and the search for extraterrestrial life, as detecting atmospheres on rocky planets was previously limited to gas giants or extremely hot worlds. It paves the way for future telescopic studies aimed at identifying biosignatures and assessing planetary habitability. The planet is roughly 5.6 times Earth's mass and 70 percent larger in radius, with models suggesting it may be an ocean world containing up to 19 percent water by mass. Scientists detected the atmosphere primarily by observing escaping helium during transit observations, leveraging LHS 1140b's favorable alignment with its host star.

rss · Hackaday · Jul 20, 11:00

**Background**: Exoplanets are planets orbiting stars outside our solar system, and astronomers typically detect their atmospheres using the transit method, where starlight filters through a planet's upper atmosphere as it passes in front of its host star. Previous atmospheric detections have largely focused on hot Jupiters or hot Neptunes because their swollen, heated atmospheres are easier to analyze spectrally. Rocky planets in the habitable zone, where liquid water could exist, have been much harder to study due to their smaller size and thinner atmospheric signatures.

## [Measuring AI Writing Across arXiv Papers and Where Detection Breaks](https://unslop.run/blog/measuring-ai-writing-on-arxiv) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A longitudinal analysis of 12,750 arXiv papers from 2021 to 2026 found that approximately 39% were flagged as AI-written by January 2026, with computer science peaking at 65% while mathematics remained near 0.7%. This finding underscores the rapid integration of large language models into academic publishing, particularly in computer science, while highlighting ongoing concerns about research integrity and detector reliability. The researcher tuned the detection system to minimize false positives, establishing a pre-ChatGPT baseline of roughly 0.4%, though critics question the methodology and note that no source code was provided for independent reproduction.

**Background**: arXiv serves as a major platform for sharing academic papers before formal publication. This study applies those detectors across a five-year window spanning the release of ChatGPT to track disciplinary differences in AI-assisted writing.

## [Study Finds Two-Thirds of Apps for US Troops Embed Chinese and Russian Code](https://www.wired.com/story/apps-marketed-to-us-troops-are-shipping-chinese-and-russian-code/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Purdue University researchers found that nearly two-thirds of over 220 apps marketed to US military personnel contain third-party code from China or Russia, including Huawei SDKs. While no data exfiltration has been confirmed, the SDKs can be remotely updated, creating a latent risk of activation. This highlights a critical supply chain security vulnerability within the US military ecosystem, as adversarial nations could potentially exploit embedded code to access sensitive user data or monitor personnel. The finding intensifies national security debates regarding the reliance on foreign technology components in defense-adjacent applications. The study noted no observed data flow to Huawei servers, but emphasized that SDKs can receive remote updates which could activate dormant code. Surveys of 103 military-affiliated individuals revealed 76% to 83% felt extremely uneasy about code from adversarial nations being present in these apps.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 20, 13:42

**Background**: Third-party SDKs are software components integrated into mobile applications to provide functionality like analytics, advertising, or authentication without requiring developers to build these features from scratch. Supply chain risks arise because SDKs often operate with broad permissions inherited from the host app and can be updated dynamically after deployment, making it difficult to monitor what code is actually running on a device. The inclusion of Huawei SDKs is particularly sensitive given US government restrictions and concerns about potential ties between Chinese tech firms and state intelligence operations.

## [High-Risk No-Gadget RCE Vulnerability Discovered in Unmaintained Fastjson 1.x](https://x.com/k_firsov/status/2078872293745570032) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Security researcher Kirill Firsov disclosed that Fastjson versions 1.2.68 through 1.2.83 contain a high-risk remote code execution vulnerability that requires neither autoTypeSupport nor classpath gadgets to exploit across JDK 8, 17, and 21. Since Fastjson 1.x reached end-of-life in October 2024, the official team is unlikely to release a patch, making migration to Fastjson2 the only recommended fix. This advisory is critical for Java developers relying on legacy Fastjson, as the novel no-gadget attack vector bypasses traditional deserialization defenses and affects widely deployed environments running supported JDK versions. The end-of-life status of the library means organizations must take immediate action to prevent potential remote code execution attacks without waiting for upstream fixes. The vulnerability can be triggered without enabling autoTypeSupport and does not depend on specific gadgets present in the classpath, simplifying exploitation compared to previous Fastjson flaws. Users are advised to immediately audit their dependencies and either upgrade to Fastjson2 or enable SafeMode via startup parameters and configuration files.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 20, 14:32

**Background**: Fastjson is a widely used Java library for parsing and generating JSON data, but its older 1.x branch has been end-of-life since October 2024. In Java security, gadget chains refer to sequences of existing objects in an application's classpath that can be chained together during deserialization to execute arbitrary commands. Previous Fastjson vulnerabilities often relied on enabling AutoType or finding specific gadgets, but this new flaw demonstrates a dangerous path where neither prerequisite is necessary.
